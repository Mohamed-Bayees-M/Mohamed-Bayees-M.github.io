01About

Platforms that move safely and recover fast.

I build the infrastructure teams rely on every day: Kubernetes fleets, Terraform modules, delivery pipelines, secure access and observability.

My work sits between scale, safety and speed. I have moved production databases and multi-TB datasets from AWS to GCP with minimal or zero downtime, and cut cloud costs by about 30% along the way.

I like delivery that is reviewable and repeatable: GitOps with ArgoCD, Terraform that is checked in CI with Trivy and Checkov, and access that is audited through Teleport and keyless authentication.

When something breaks I want to know why, so I instrument with Datadog, OpenTelemetry and the Grafana stack, and take part in on-call and root cause analysis to stop repeats.

If a deploy, migration or incident needs luck, the platform is not finished.
